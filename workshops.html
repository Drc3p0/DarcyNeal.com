Hands-on electronics, taught in person

Soldering and circuit workshops run at hacker cons, maker fairs, and artist residencies worldwide, scaled from small studio groups to 50-student sessions.

EMF Explorer Workshop

Build a wearable EMF sensing badge, then take it on a sound exploration walk to find electromagnetic signals in the wild. Scales to 50 students at a time.

DEF CON · HOPE · ToorCamp · dadaLab · Open Hardware Summit · Chaos Communication Congress · Bay Area Maker Faire

Pathlighter Soldering Workshop

A first-solder-friendly build, about 20 minutes start to finish, using the board’s own fabrication layers as the art. Has reached over 450 students to date.

ToorCamp 2022 · Chaos Communication Camp 2023

Freeform LED Sculpture Workshop

Illuminated sculptures built with no PCB or breadboard: wire and components bent directly into three-dimensional form. Comes with an accompanying zine.

Arai-Eek residency, Chiang Mai · Open Hardware Summit, Berlin

Creative PCB Design with KiCad

Importing custom artwork into PCB design software, treating soldermask and silkscreen as art layers, and using multicolor UV silkscreen for full-color detail.

Portland State University · PDX Hackerspace · WHY2025, Netherlands · 39C3, Germany · BornHack, Denmark · Chaihuo Makerspace, Shenzhen
