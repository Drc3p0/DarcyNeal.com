Darcy Neal

Portland, OR
photo: portrait — add file to images/about/

Darcy is an interactive audio-visual hardware designer and educator with fifteen-plus years of production experience, spanning gallery installations, live events, and manufactured products. Work draws on a background rooted in the hacker and maker community: animatronic fabrication, boutique synthesizer production at 4MS Company, and five years directing Portland Community College’s makerspace.

Founder of SporkLogic, a DIY electronics kit company built around collectible PCB art and teaching tools like the EMF Explorer. Also works as Campaign Project Manager at Crowd Supply, helping open-source hardware creators launch and fund their own products.

Teaching runs through everything: workshops at DEF CON, HOPE, ToorCamp, Chaos Communication Congress, Open Hardware Summit, and Bay Area Maker Faire. Recent residencies include Research at Scale in Shenzhen (Chaihuo Makerspace, MIT Media Lab), an artist residency at Sou’Wester, and Arai-Eek in Chiang Mai.
